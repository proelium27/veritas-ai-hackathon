## What we hand in

1. **Code:** a Colab notebook or GitHub repo the judges can open and run.
2. **Write-up:** one page on the problem, our approach, and how well it worked.
3. **Demo video:** 2 minutes, unlisted YouTube link or direct upload.

**Where to submit:** the Airtable form at https://airtable.com/appypCyHN77hElIcS/shrXA5VBFdugmoXl6 (linked from highschoolhackathon.org/briefs). One person submits for the team; resubmit as often as needed, the latest version counts. Contact for submission problems only: contact@veritasai.com.

## Rules

- All code is written during the 48 hours.
- AI tools are fine for debugging, explaining and routine code, but all three of us must be able to explain every part.
- Public datasets and pre-trained models are OK if credited.
- Credit every dataset, library and model we use.

## Judging

Round 1 is out of 100 points and counts for 30% of the final ranking. Live finals count for 70%.

| Criterion | Points | How to score |
|---|---|---|
| Technical execution | 30 | It runs and shows results in numbers |
| Originality | 20 | Our own angle, not a tutorial copy |
| Impact and track fit | 20 | Clear who it helps, tied to the challenge |
| Testing and honesty | 15 | Show how we tested it and where it fails |
| Communication | 15 | Clear write-up and video; all of us can explain it |

Round 1 is scored blind by the Veritas AI team (no names or school shown). Finalists are announced Wed Nov 18.

**Finals rubric (100 points, new panel):** Demo and clarity 30, Technical depth 30, Impact 20, Q&A 20. Format: 5-minute live demo on a public livestream, then panel questions.
