# What Caleb shares with teammates, and how

Times are Eastern. "Group chat" means whatever chat the team picks.

## Tonight (Fri Nov 13), before 11:59 PM

| Share | How |
|---|---|
| Registration | Make sure both teammates are on the team's registration at highschoolhackathon.org/register (it asks for teammates' names and emails). Ask each of them to confirm they got a registration email. |
| Readiness guide | Open https://claude.ai/artifact/4dAfn821v58LusAZRBhdEj, click **Share**, and give both teammates access (by email, or anyone with the link). Send the link in the group chat. Ask them to finish the "Do these tonight" and "Accounts" boxes. |
| Ask for usernames | Post in the chat: "Send me your GitHub username, and check your inbox and spam for the hackathon starter kit email." |
| Call link | Post one video-call link for kickoff and say when you're meeting (midnight, or Sat morning). |

## At kickoff (Sat Nov 14, 12:00 AM, or Sat morning)

| Share | How |
|---|---|
| Challenge brief | Whoever sees the email first forwards it to the group chat, and to the commander session (paste it in). |
| Starter kit and submission info | Forward the starter kit email to the chat. The commander adds the submission details to docs/competition.md. |
| GitHub repo | The commander creates the private repo and sends invites to the usernames. Tell teammates: "Accept the GitHub invite email, then check the repo opens." |
| The plan | Once the idea is picked, share the decision in the chat: track, idea, dataset link, who does what, next check-in time. The commander writes it into docs/plan.md in the repo. |
| Notebook link | Teammate 2 (notebook owner) opens `notebook/model.ipynb` from GitHub in Colab. Everyone else makes their own copy (File › Save a copy in Drive). |

## During the weekend

- Post "I'm editing the main notebook" / "Done, saved" before and after every save to the main notebook.
- Post real results (scores, failures) in the chat as they happen; the commander logs them.
- Check-ins: Sat afternoon, Sat evening, Sun morning, Sun afternoon (times in docs/plan.md).

## Sunday evening (submission)

| Share | How |
|---|---|
| Write-up draft | Shared Google Doc, all three edit; final copy goes in `docs/writeup.md`. |
| Video | Teammate 3 uploads to YouTube as **Unlisted** and posts the link in the chat. |
| Final links | Caleb makes the repo public (Settings › General › Change visibility), then posts the repo link and the Colab link. Everyone opens them in a signed-out private window to check. |
| Submission | One person submits by 10:59 PM and posts a screenshot of the confirmation in the chat. |

## Never share

- Hugging Face or any other token or password, anywhere: chat, notebook, repo, write-up, screenshots.
- Names or school in the notebook, write-up or video (Round 1 is judged blind).
